\section*{Article I. Name}
The official name of this organization shall be the \textbf{\orgname} (hereafter, the ``Organization'').

\section*{Article II. Mission and Purpose}
The Organization advances student scholarship in artificial intelligence and machine learning by:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item Hosting regular reading sessions of peer-reviewed AI/ML papers with critical discussion;
    \item Developing members’ research, presentation, and replication skills;
    \item Facilitating interdisciplinary collaboration across departments and labs;
    \item Inviting authors and practitioners for talks, panels, and workshops;
    \item Promoting ethical, responsible, and reproducible AI/ML research practices.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Article III. Non-Discrimination}
The Organization shall not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, ethnicity, age, disability, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, pregnancy, religion, veteran status, genetic information, or any other status protected by law or \university{} policy.

\section*{Article IV. Membership}
\subsection*{Section 1. Eligibility}
Membership is open to all currently enrolled \university{} students (undergraduate, graduate, professional). Faculty and staff may participate as non-voting affiliates.

\subsection*{Section 2. Good Standing}
A member is in good standing if they:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item Attend at least two Organization meetings/events per academic term; and
    \item Adhere to this Constitution, bylaws, and \university{} policies; and
    \item Maintain professional and respectful conduct.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Section 3. Dues}
No dues are required by default. If needed for programming, the Comptroller may propose dues in the bylaws, subject to approval by a majority vote of members present with quorum.

\section*{Article V. Officers and Duties}
The elected officers shall be: \textbf{President}, \textbf{Vice President}, \textbf{Comptroller}, \textbf{Public Relations Officer}, and \textbf{Secretary}. Officers must be student members in good standing.

\subsection*{Section 1. President}
Leads the Organization; sets agendas; presides over meetings; ensures execution of decisions; serves as primary liaison to \university{} offices and the Faculty/Staff Advisor; signs official documents; oversees risk management and compliance.

\subsection*{Section 2. Vice President}
Supports the President; coordinates programming (reading schedules, discussants, workshops); manages committees; assumes presidential duties when the President is absent or if the office becomes vacant until succession/election occurs.

\subsection*{Section 3. Comptroller}
Manages finances; maintains accurate ledgers; prepares budgets and funding requests; oversees reimbursements; provides a financial report at least once per term; ensures compliance with \university{} fiscal policies.

\subsection*{Section 4. Public Relations Officer}
Leads outreach and communications; manages the website, email lists, and social media; coordinates event promotion; cultivates partnerships with related groups and departments.

\subsection*{Section 5. Secretary}
Maintains records (minutes, attendance, archives); manages membership rolls; handles official correspondence; maintains shared repositories (e.g., reading lists, slides, code).

\subsection*{Section 6. Advisor}
The Organization shall maintain at least one \university{} Faculty/Staff Advisor who provides guidance and continuity. The Advisor is non-voting.

\section*{Article VI. Terms, Elections, and Vacancies}
\subsection*{Section 1. Terms}
Officer terms run from \textbf{\termbegin} to \textbf{\termend}. Officers may be re-elected without term limits unless restricted by bylaws or \university{} policy.

\subsection*{Section 2. First Cohort (Initial Appointments)}
To expedite startup, the \textbf{initial officers} (President, Vice President, Comptroller, Public Relations Officer, Secretary) may be \textbf{appointed} by the founding members and Advisor. These appointees serve until the first regular election in \electionmonth{} (or sooner if the Organization opts to hold elections earlier).

\subsection*{Section 3. Nominations and Ballots}
At least two weeks before the \electionmonth{} meeting, the Secretary solicits nominations (self-nominations permitted). Ballots may be conducted by secure electronic vote or in person. Votes are cast by members in good standing.

\subsection*{Section 4. Voting and Quorum}
Quorum for elections and officer removal is \textbf{\quorum} of members in good standing. Officers are elected by a simple majority of votes cast. Ties are resolved by a runoff between tied candidates.

\subsection*{Section 5. Vacancies and Succession}
If the Presidency becomes vacant, the Vice President serves as Acting President until a special election is held within 30 days. Other vacancies are filled by special election within 30 days. The Acting Officer may run in the special election.

\subsection*{Section 6. Removal}
An officer may be removed for failure to perform duties, misconduct, or policy violations. Removal requires:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item Written motion signed by at least 20\% of members in good standing; and
    \item Notice to the officer at least 7 days in advance; and
    \item A two-thirds vote of members present with quorum.
\end{enumerate}
The officer has the right to speak before the vote.

\section*{Article VII. Meetings}
\subsection*{Section 1. Regular Meetings}
The Organization shall hold regular meetings (e.g., weekly or biweekly during academic terms) for paper discussions and business as needed. The President (or designee) circulates agendas at least 24 hours in advance when feasible.

\subsection*{Section 2. Special Meetings}
Special meetings may be called by the President, a majority of officers, or the Advisor with at least 48 hours’ notice when practical.

\subsection*{Section 3. Decision-Making}
Unless otherwise specified, decisions require a simple majority of members present with quorum.

\section*{Article VIII. Committees}
Standing or ad hoc committees may be established by the President with officer approval. Suggested standing committees include:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item \textit{Reading and Replication Committee}: curates papers, leads replications/code walkthroughs;
    \item \textit{Events and Outreach Committee}: coordinates speakers, workshops, and collaborations.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Article IX. Finance}
All funds shall be used to advance the Organization’s mission and managed under \university{} policies. The Comptroller must maintain transparent records and provide reports at least once per term. Expenditures require prior officer approval per bylaws (e.g., majority of officers for expenses over a set threshold). Upon dissolution, remaining funds revert as required by \university{} policy.

\section*{Article X. Parliamentary Authority}
The Organization may use \textit{Robert’s Rules of Order Newly Revised} for guidance where consistent with this Constitution and bylaws.

\section*{Article XI. Amendments}
\subsection*{Section 1. Proposal}
Any member in good standing may propose an amendment by submitting it in writing to the Secretary.

\subsection*{Section 2. Notice and Adoption}
Amendments require (i) at least one week of advance notice to membership and (ii) a two-thirds vote of members present with quorum at a regular or special meeting.

\section*{Article XII. Bylaws}
\subsection*{Section 1. Purpose}
Bylaws provide operational detail (e.g., detailed election timelines, committee charters, officer handbooks, dues amounts, conflict-of-interest procedures).

\subsection*{Section 2. Adoption and Revision}
Bylaws and revisions may be adopted by a majority vote of members present with quorum, provided at least one week’s notice is given.

\section*{Article XIII. Compliance}
This Organization will comply with all applicable local, state, and federal laws; \university{} policies; and the requirements of the \university{} student organization recognition process (see: \url{https://www.augusta.edu/student-life/organizations/start-new-organization.php}).

\section*{Article XIV. Dissolution}
Upon dissolution, all debts and liabilities shall be settled in accordance with \university{} policy. Remaining assets shall be transferred as directed by \university{} guidelines for student organizations.
