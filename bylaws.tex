\section*{Article I. Purpose}
These Bylaws provide operational detail for the governance and administration of the \orgname{} (hereafter, the ``Organization''), supplementing the Constitution.

\section*{Article II. Membership}
\subsection*{Section 1. Good Standing}
A member remains in good standing if they:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item Attend at least two meetings or events per academic term;
    \item Participate respectfully in discussions; and
    \item Remain enrolled at \university{} (students) or maintain recognized affiliate status (faculty/staff).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Section 2. Suspension of Membership}
Members who fail to maintain good standing may be suspended from voting privileges by a majority of officers until standing is restored.

\section*{Article III. Elections}
\subsection*{Section 1. Timing}
Elections shall occur each year in \electionmonth{}. New terms begin on \termbegin{} and end on \termend{}.

\subsection*{Section 2. Nominations}
The Secretary shall solicit officer nominations at least two weeks before the election meeting. Nominations may be submitted in writing or electronically. Self-nominations are allowed.

\subsection*{Section 3. Ballot and Voting}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item Elections may be conducted via secret ballot (paper or secure electronic system).
    \item Quorum for officer elections is \quorum{} of members in good standing.
    \item Officers are elected by simple majority of votes cast.
    \item In case of a tie, a runoff is held immediately between the tied candidates.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Section 4. Vacancies}
Special elections shall be held within 30 days of any officer vacancy. Interim officers may be appointed by majority vote of the remaining officers until the special election.

\section*{Article IV. Duties of Officers}
Officers shall fulfill duties as described in the Constitution. Additional operational expectations include:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item \textbf{President:} Circulates meeting agendas 48 hours in advance; files an annual report to the Office of Student Life.
    \item \textbf{Vice President:} Ensures a balanced mix of theory and applied AI/ML papers are scheduled each term.
    \item \textbf{Comptroller:} Keeps financial ledgers up to date within 14 days of any transaction; coordinates with Student Life for funding opportunities.
    \item \textbf{Public Relations Officer:} Posts at least one update (event announcement, highlight, or recap) per month on official channels.
    \item \textbf{Secretary:} Uploads minutes to a shared archive within 7 days of meetings.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Article V. Meetings}
\subsection*{Section 1. Regular Meetings}
The Organization shall hold at least two meetings per month during the academic year for paper discussion and organizational business.

\subsection*{Section 2. Special Meetings}
Special meetings may be called by the President, a majority of officers, or the Advisor with at least 48 hours’ notice.

\subsection*{Section 3. Quorum}
Quorum for meetings is defined as \quorum{} of active members in good standing.

\section*{Article VI. Committees}
\subsection*{Section 1. Establishment}
Committees may be established by majority vote of the officers. Each committee shall have a designated Chair.

\subsection*{Section 2. Standing Committees}
The following standing committees are recommended:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item \textbf{Reading and Replication Committee:} Curates paper lists, assigns discussion leaders, and organizes replication/code sessions.
    \item \textbf{Events and Outreach Committee:} Coordinates guest speakers, joint events, and external partnerships.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Section 3. Reporting}
Committee Chairs must provide an oral or written report at least once per academic term.

\section*{Article VII. Finance}
\subsection*{Section 1. Budget}
The Comptroller shall propose a budget each academic year for officer approval and membership review.

\subsection*{Section 2. Expenditures}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item Routine expenditures under \$100 may be approved by the Comptroller and one other officer.
    \item Expenditures over \$100 require majority approval of officers.
    \item All reimbursements must comply with \university{} policies and require receipts.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Section 3. Transparency}
The Comptroller shall present a financial statement to the membership at least once per academic term.

\section*{Article VIII. Amendments to Bylaws}
\subsection*{Section 1. Proposal}
Any member may propose an amendment to these Bylaws.

\subsection*{Section 2. Adoption}
Amendments to the Bylaws require:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
    \item At least one week of advance notice to membership; and
    \item A simple majority vote of members present with quorum.
\end{enumerate}
